### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Solar Pro 4?
Solar Pro 4 is an AI language model from Upstage. It is a proprietary model, available through an API. It scores 41.6 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Solar Pro 4 free?
Solar Pro 4 is not free: it costs $0.03 per million input tokens and $0.12 per million output tokens. Open-weight alternatives can be self-hosted at no per-token cost.

#### What is Solar Pro 4 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 89.1%, Humanity's Last Exam 29.2%, Long Context Reasoning 70.7%, SciCode 43.6%, τ-Bench Banking 23.3%, Terminal-Bench 57.3%. It is particularly used for code generation.

#### How fast is Solar Pro 4?
It generates about 51.1 tokens per second, with a median 1.22s delay before the first token. Measured independently by Artificial Analysis.
