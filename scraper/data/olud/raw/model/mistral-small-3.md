### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Mistral Small 3?
Mistral Small 3 is an AI language model from Mistral AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 6.7 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Mistral Small 3 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Mistral Small 3 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.05 per million input tokens and $0.08 per million output tokens.
