#### What is Qwen3.7 Max?
Qwen3.7 Max is an AI language model from Alibaba. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 46.7 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

The weights are free and open — you can self-host Qwen3.7 Max and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $1.48 per million input tokens and $4.43 per million output tokens.
