### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Qwen3 32B?
Qwen3 32B is an AI language model from Alibaba. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 11.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Qwen3 32B free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Qwen3 32B and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.08 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens.

#### What is Qwen3 32B good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 66.8%, MMLU-Pro 79.8%, Humanity's Last Exam 7.4%, Long Context Reasoning 0%, LiveCodeBench 54.6%, SciCode 35.4%, MATH-500 96.1%, AIME 80.7%, AIME 2025 73%, IFBench 36.3%, τ²-Bench 29.8%, τ-Bench Banking 5.4%, Terminal-Bench 5.2%, Terminal-Bench Hard 3%.
