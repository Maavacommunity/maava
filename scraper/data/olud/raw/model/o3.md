### Frequently asked questions

#### What is o3?
o3 is an AI language model from OpenAI. It is a proprietary model, available through an API. It scores 31.1 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is o3 free?
o3 is not free: it costs $2 per million input tokens and $8 per million output tokens. Open-weight alternatives can be self-hosted at no per-token cost.

#### What is o3 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 82.7%, MMLU-Pro 85.3%, Humanity's Last Exam 20.1%, Long Context Reasoning 73.3%, LiveCodeBench 80.8%, SciCode 41%, MATH-500 99.2%, AIME 90.3%, AIME 2025 88.3%, IFBench 71.4%, τ²-Bench 80.7%, Terminal-Bench Hard 37.1%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.
