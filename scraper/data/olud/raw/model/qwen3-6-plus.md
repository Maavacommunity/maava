### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Qwen3.6 Plus?
Qwen3.6 Plus is an AI language model from Alibaba. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 40.5 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Qwen3.6 Plus free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Qwen3.6 Plus and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.33 per million input tokens and $1.95 per million output tokens.

#### What is Qwen3.6 Plus good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 88.2%, Humanity's Last Exam 27.8%, Long Context Reasoning 72.3%, SciCode 40.7%, IFBench 75.2%, τ²-Bench 97.7%, τ-Bench Banking 20.8%, Terminal-Bench 61.4%, Terminal-Bench Hard 43.9%. It is particularly used for code generation.

#### Can I self-host Qwen3.6 Plus?
Yes. Qwen3.6 Plus has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
