### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Hermes 4 405B?
Hermes 4 405B is an AI language model from Nous Research. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 8.8 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Hermes 4 405B free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Hermes 4 405B and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $1 per million input tokens and $3 per million output tokens.

#### What is Hermes 4 405B good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 72.7%, MMLU-Pro 82.9%, Humanity's Last Exam 10.9%, Long Context Reasoning 22.7%, LiveCodeBench 68.6%, SciCode 25.2%, AIME 2025 69.7%, IFBench 32.7%, τ²-Bench 22.2%, Terminal-Bench Hard 11.4%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### How fast is Hermes 4 405B?
It generates about 34 tokens per second, with a median 0.92s delay before the first token. Measured independently by Artificial Analysis.

#### Can I self-host Hermes 4 405B?
Yes. Hermes 4 405B has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
