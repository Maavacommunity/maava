### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GLM 4.6?
GLM 4.6 is an AI language model from Z.AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 23.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GLM 4.6 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host GLM 4.6 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.43 per million input tokens and $1.75 per million output tokens.

#### What is GLM 4.6 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 63.2%, MMLU-Pro 78.4%, Humanity's Last Exam 5.5%, Long Context Reasoning 28.3%, LiveCodeBench 56.1%, SciCode 33.1%, AIME 2025 44.3%, IFBench 36.7%, τ²-Bench 76.9%, Terminal-Bench Hard 28.8%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host GLM 4.6?
Yes. GLM 4.6 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
