### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Step 3.5 Flash?
Step 3.5 Flash is an AI language model from Stepfun. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 26.5 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Step 3.5 Flash free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Step 3.5 Flash and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.1 per million input tokens and $0.3 per million output tokens.
