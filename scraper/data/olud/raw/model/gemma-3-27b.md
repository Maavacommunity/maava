### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Gemma 3 27B?
Gemma 3 27B is an AI language model from Google. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 7.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Gemma 3 27B free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Gemma 3 27B and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.08 per million input tokens and $0.45 per million output tokens.
