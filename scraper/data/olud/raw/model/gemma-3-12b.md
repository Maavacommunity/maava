### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Gemma 3 12B?
Gemma 3 12B is an AI language model from Google. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 5.5 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Gemma 3 12B free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Gemma 3 12B and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.05 per million input tokens and $0.15 per million output tokens.
