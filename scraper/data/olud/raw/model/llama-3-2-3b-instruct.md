### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Llama 3.2 3B Instruct?
Llama 3.2 3B Instruct is an AI language model from Meta. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 3.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Llama 3.2 3B Instruct free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Llama 3.2 3B Instruct and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.05 per million input tokens and $0.33 per million output tokens.
