### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GLM 5?
GLM 5 is an AI language model from Z.AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 40.6 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GLM 5 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host GLM 5 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.6 per million input tokens and $1.92 per million output tokens.

#### What is GLM 5 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 82%, Humanity's Last Exam 29.3%, Long Context Reasoning 70.7%, SciCode 46.2%, IFBench 72.3%, τ²-Bench 98.2%, Terminal-Bench Hard 43.2%.

#### Can I self-host GLM 5?
Yes. GLM 5 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
