### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Qwen3.7 Plus?
Qwen3.7 Plus is an AI language model from Alibaba. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 39.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Qwen3.7 Plus free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Qwen3.7 Plus and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.32 per million input tokens and $1.28 per million output tokens.

#### What is Qwen3.7 Plus good at?

#### How fast is Qwen3.7 Plus?

#### Can I self-host Qwen3.7 Plus?
Yes. Qwen3.7 Plus has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
