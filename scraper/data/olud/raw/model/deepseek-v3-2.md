### Frequently asked questions

#### What is DeepSeek V3.2?
DeepSeek V3.2 is an AI language model from DeepSeek. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 25.1 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is DeepSeek V3.2 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host DeepSeek V3.2 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.26 per million input tokens and $0.38 per million output tokens.
