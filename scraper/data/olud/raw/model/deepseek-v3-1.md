### Frequently asked questions

#### What is DeepSeek V3.1?
DeepSeek V3.1 is an AI language model from DeepSeek. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 21.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is DeepSeek V3.1 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host DeepSeek V3.1 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.55 per million input tokens and $1.65 per million output tokens.

#### What is DeepSeek V3.1 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 73.5%, MMLU-Pro 83.3%, Humanity's Last Exam 6.7%, Long Context Reasoning 46.7%, LiveCodeBench 57.7%, SciCode 36.7%, AIME 2025 49.7%, IFBench 37.8%, τ²-Bench 34.8%, Terminal-Bench Hard 24.2%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host DeepSeek V3.1?
Yes. DeepSeek V3.1 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
