### Frequently asked questions

#### What is gpt-oss-20b?
gpt-oss-20b is an AI language model from OpenAI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 15.2 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is gpt-oss-20b free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host gpt-oss-20b and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.03 per million input tokens and $0.13 per million output tokens.
