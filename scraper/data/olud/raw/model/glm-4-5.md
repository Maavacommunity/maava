### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GLM 4.5?
GLM 4.5 is an AI language model from Z.AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 19.7 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GLM 4.5 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host GLM 4.5 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.6 per million input tokens and $2.2 per million output tokens.

#### What is GLM 4.5 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 78.2%, MMLU-Pro 83.5%, Humanity's Last Exam 13%, Long Context Reasoning 51.7%, LiveCodeBench 73.8%, SciCode 34.8%, MATH-500 97.9%, AIME 87.3%, AIME 2025 73.7%, IFBench 44.1%, τ²-Bench 43%, Terminal-Bench Hard 22%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host GLM 4.5?
Yes. GLM 4.5 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
