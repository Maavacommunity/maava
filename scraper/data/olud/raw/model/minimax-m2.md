### Frequently asked questions

#### What is MiniMax M2?
MiniMax M2 is an AI language model from MiniMax. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 28.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is MiniMax M2 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host MiniMax M2 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.26 per million input tokens and $1.02 per million output tokens.

#### What is MiniMax M2 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 77.7%, MMLU-Pro 82%, Humanity's Last Exam 13.7%, Long Context Reasoning 65%, LiveCodeBench 82.6%, SciCode 36.1%, AIME 2025 78.3%, IFBench 72.3%, τ²-Bench 86.8%, Terminal-Bench Hard 25.8%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host MiniMax M2?
Yes. MiniMax M2 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
