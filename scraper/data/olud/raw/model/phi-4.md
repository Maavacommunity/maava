### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Phi 4?
Phi 4 is an AI language model from Microsoft. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 4.6 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Phi 4 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Phi 4 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.14 per million output tokens.

#### What is Phi 4 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 57.5%, MMLU-Pro 71.4%, Humanity's Last Exam 3.8%, Long Context Reasoning 0%, LiveCodeBench 23.1%, SciCode 26%, MATH-500 81%, AIME 14.3%, AIME 2025 18%, IFBench 23.5%, τ²-Bench 0%, Terminal-Bench Hard 3.8%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### How fast is Phi 4?
It generates about 43.4 tokens per second, with a median 0.93s delay before the first token. Measured independently by Artificial Analysis.

#### Can I self-host Phi 4?
Yes. Phi 4 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
