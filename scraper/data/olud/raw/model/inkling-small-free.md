### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Inkling Small (free)?
Inkling Small (free) is an AI language model from Thinkingmachines. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 41.2 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Inkling Small (free) free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Inkling Small (free) and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are Free per million input tokens and Free per million output tokens.

#### What is Inkling Small (free) good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 89.5%, Humanity's Last Exam 33.3%, Long Context Reasoning 69.3%, SciCode 48.7%, τ-Bench Banking 18.8%, Terminal-Bench 55.1%. It is particularly used for code generation.

#### How fast is Inkling Small (free)?
It generates about 61.4 tokens per second, with a median 2.19s delay before the first token. Measured independently by Artificial Analysis.

#### Can I self-host Inkling Small (free)?
Yes. Inkling Small (free) has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
