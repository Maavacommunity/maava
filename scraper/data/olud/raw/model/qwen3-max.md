### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Qwen3 Max?
Qwen3 Max is an AI language model from Alibaba. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 24.5 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Qwen3 Max free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Qwen3 Max and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.78 per million input tokens and $3.9 per million output tokens.

#### What is Qwen3 Max good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 76.4%, MMLU-Pro 84.1%, Humanity's Last Exam 11.9%, Long Context Reasoning 48.3%, LiveCodeBench 76.7%, SciCode 38.3%, AIME 2025 80.7%, IFBench 44.1%, τ²-Bench 74.3%, Terminal-Bench Hard 20.5%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host Qwen3 Max?
Yes. Qwen3 Max has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
