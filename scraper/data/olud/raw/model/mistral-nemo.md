### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Mistral Nemo?
Mistral Nemo is an AI language model from Mistral AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free.

#### Is Mistral Nemo free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Mistral Nemo and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.02 per million input tokens and $0.03 per million output tokens.

#### Can I self-host Mistral Nemo?
Yes. Mistral Nemo has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
