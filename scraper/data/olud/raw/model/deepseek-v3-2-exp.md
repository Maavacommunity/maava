### Frequently asked questions

#### What is DeepSeek V3.2 Exp?
DeepSeek V3.2 Exp is an AI language model from DeepSeek. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 25.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is DeepSeek V3.2 Exp free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host DeepSeek V3.2 Exp and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.27 per million input tokens and $0.41 per million output tokens.

#### What is DeepSeek V3.2 Exp good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 79.7%, MMLU-Pro 85%, Humanity's Last Exam 14.9%, Long Context Reasoning 72.3%, LiveCodeBench 78.9%, SciCode 37.7%, AIME 2025 87.7%, IFBench 54.1%, τ²-Bench 33.9%, Terminal-Bench Hard 31.1%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host DeepSeek V3.2 Exp?
