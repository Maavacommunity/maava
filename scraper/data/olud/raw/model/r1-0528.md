### Frequently asked questions

#### What is R1 0528?
R1 0528 is an AI language model from DeepSeek. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 20.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is R1 0528 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host R1 0528 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.5 per million input tokens and $2.15 per million output tokens.

#### What is R1 0528 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 81.3%, MMLU-Pro 84.9%, Humanity's Last Exam 15.8%, Long Context Reasoning 56.7%, LiveCodeBench 77%, SciCode 40.3%, MATH-500 98.3%, AIME 89.3%, AIME 2025 76%, IFBench 39.6%, τ²-Bench 36.5%, Terminal-Bench Hard 15.9%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host R1 0528?
