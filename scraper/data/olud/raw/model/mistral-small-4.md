### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Mistral Small 4?
Mistral Small 4 is an AI language model from Mistral AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 19.7 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Mistral Small 4 free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Mistral Small 4 and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.6 per million output tokens.

#### What is Mistral Small 4 good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 76.9%, Humanity's Last Exam 9.9%, Long Context Reasoning 47.3%, SciCode 38%, IFBench 48.2%, τ²-Bench 41.2%, τ-Bench Banking 4.9%, Terminal-Bench 21%, Terminal-Bench Hard 17.4%. It is particularly used for code generation.

#### How fast is Mistral Small 4?
It generates about 164.4 tokens per second, with a median 0.67s delay before the first token. Measured independently by Artificial Analysis.

#### Can I self-host Mistral Small 4?
Yes. Mistral Small 4 has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
