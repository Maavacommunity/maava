### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Qwen3.5-9B?
Qwen3.5-9B is an AI language model from Alibaba. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 21.8 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Qwen3.5-9B free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Qwen3.5-9B and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.1 per million input tokens and $0.15 per million output tokens.

#### What is Qwen3.5-9B good at?

#### Can I self-host Qwen3.5-9B?
