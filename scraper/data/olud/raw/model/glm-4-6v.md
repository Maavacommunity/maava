### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GLM 4.6V?
GLM 4.6V is an AI language model from Z.AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 10.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GLM 4.6V free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host GLM 4.6V and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.3 per million input tokens and $0.9 per million output tokens.

#### What is GLM 4.6V good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 56.6%, MMLU-Pro 75.2%, Humanity's Last Exam 3.7%, Long Context Reasoning 14.3%, LiveCodeBench 41.1%, SciCode 27.2%, AIME 2025 26.3%, IFBench 27.9%, τ²-Bench 30.7%, Terminal-Bench Hard 3%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host GLM 4.6V?
Yes. GLM 4.6V has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
