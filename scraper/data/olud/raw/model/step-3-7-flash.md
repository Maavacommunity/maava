#### What is Step 3.7 Flash?
Step 3.7 Flash is an AI language model from Stepfun. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 30.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

The weights are free and open — you can self-host Step 3.7 Flash and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.2 per million input tokens and $1.15 per million output tokens.
