### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GPT-4o?
GPT-4o is an AI language model from OpenAI. It is a proprietary model, available through an API. It scores 12.3 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GPT-4o free?
GPT-4o is not free: it costs $2.5 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Open-weight alternatives can be self-hosted at no per-token cost.

#### What is GPT-4o good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 65.5%, MMLU-Pro 80.3%, Humanity's Last Exam 4%, LiveCodeBench 42.5%, SciCode 36.6%, MATH-500 89.3%, AIME 32.7%, AIME 2025 25.7%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.
