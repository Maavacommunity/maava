### Frequently asked questions

#### What is o4 Mini?
o4 Mini is an AI language model from OpenAI. It is a proprietary model, available through an API. It scores 26.1 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is o4 Mini free?
o4 Mini is not free: it costs $1.1 per million input tokens and $4.4 per million output tokens. Open-weight alternatives can be self-hosted at no per-token cost.
