### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GLM 5V Turbo?
GLM 5V Turbo is an AI language model from Z.AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 35.3 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GLM 5V Turbo free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host GLM 5V Turbo and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $1.2 per million input tokens and $4 per million output tokens.

#### What is GLM 5V Turbo good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 80.9%, Humanity's Last Exam 17.1%, Long Context Reasoning 65.7%, SciCode 43.5%, IFBench 61.1%, τ²-Bench 98.5%, Terminal-Bench Hard 32.6%.

#### Can I self-host GLM 5V Turbo?
Yes. GLM 5V Turbo has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
