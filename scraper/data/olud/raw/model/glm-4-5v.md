### Frequently asked questions

#### What is GLM 4.5V?
GLM 4.5V is an AI language model from Z.AI. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is GLM 4.5V free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host GLM 4.5V and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.6 per million input tokens and $1.8 per million output tokens.

#### What is GLM 4.5V good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 68.4%, MMLU-Pro 78.8%, Humanity's Last Exam 6.3%, Long Context Reasoning 0%, LiveCodeBench 60.4%, SciCode 22.1%, AIME 2025 73%, IFBench 34.2%, τ²-Bench 22.5%, Terminal-Bench Hard 5.3%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### Can I self-host GLM 4.5V?
Yes. GLM 4.5V has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
