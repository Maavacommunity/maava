### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Hermes 4 70B?
Hermes 4 70B is an AI language model from Nous Research. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 9.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Hermes 4 70B free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Hermes 4 70B and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.13 per million input tokens and $0.4 per million output tokens.

#### What is Hermes 4 70B good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 69.9%, MMLU-Pro 81.1%, Humanity's Last Exam 8.8%, Long Context Reasoning 8%, LiveCodeBench 65.3%, SciCode 34.1%, AIME 2025 68.7%, IFBench 31.3%, τ²-Bench 22.5%, Terminal-Bench Hard 4.5%. It is particularly used for mathematical reasoning.

#### How fast is Hermes 4 70B?
It generates about 86.8 tokens per second, with a median 0.6s delay before the first token. Measured independently by Artificial Analysis.

#### Can I self-host Hermes 4 70B?
Yes. Hermes 4 70B has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
