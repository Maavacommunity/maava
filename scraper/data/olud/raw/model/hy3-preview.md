### Frequently asked questions

#### What is Hy3 preview?
Hy3 preview is an AI language model from Tencent. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 34.4 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is Hy3 preview free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host Hy3 preview and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.18 per million input tokens and $0.6 per million output tokens.

#### What is Hy3 preview good at?
Independent benchmarks from Artificial Analysis give it GPQA 86.7%, Humanity's Last Exam 27.8%, Long Context Reasoning 60.3%, SciCode 41.2%, IFBench 63.1%, τ²-Bench 92.7%, Terminal-Bench Hard 34.1%.

#### Can I self-host Hy3 preview?
Yes. Hy3 preview has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
