### Frequently asked questions

#### What is MiMo-V2.5-Pro?
MiMo-V2.5-Pro is an AI language model from Xiaomi. It is open-weight: you can download it and run it on your own hardware, for free. It scores 42.9 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index.

#### Is MiMo-V2.5-Pro free?
The weights are free and open — you can self-host MiMo-V2.5-Pro and pay nothing per token. If you prefer a hosted API, list prices are $0.44 per million input tokens and $0.87 per million output tokens.

#### What is MiMo-V2.5-Pro good at?

#### Can I self-host MiMo-V2.5-Pro?
Yes. MiMo-V2.5-Pro has open weights, so you can download it and run it on your own GPU or server with tools like Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp — with no per-token cost.
