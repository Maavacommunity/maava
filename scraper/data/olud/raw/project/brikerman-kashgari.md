📄 About

Kashgari is a production-level NLP Transfer learning framework built on top of tf.keras for text-labeling and text-classification, includes Word2Vec, BERT, and GPT2 Language Embedding.

Kashgari has 2.4k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 431 times. Kashgari is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2019. Kashgari is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are bert, bert-model, gpt-2, machine-learning.
