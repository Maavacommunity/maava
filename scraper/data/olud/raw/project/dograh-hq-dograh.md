Open source voice AI platform. Self-hosted alternative to Vapi and Retell. On Prem, BYOK across Speech to Speech or LLM/STT/TTS, with a visual workflow builder, MCP native and telephony support.

dograh has 5.5k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 1.4k times. dograh is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2025. dograh is available under the BSD-2-Clause license. Its main topics are ai-calling, asterisk-ari, conversational-ai, inbound-calls.
