📄 About

MOSS-TTSD is a spoken dialogue generation model designed for expressive multi-speaker synthesis. It features long-context modeling, flexible speaker control, and multilingual support, while enabling zero-shot voice cloning from short audio references.

MOSS-TTSD has 1.4k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 136 times. MOSS-TTSD is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2025. MOSS-TTSD is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are finetune, large-language-models, sglang, speech-dialogue-generation.
