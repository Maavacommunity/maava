📄 About

Agent2Agent (A2A) is an open protocol enabling communication and interoperability between opaque agentic applications.

A2A has 25.6k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 2.6k times. A2A is written mainly in Shell. It has been in active development since 2025. A2A is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are a2a, a2a-mcp, a2a-protocol, a2a-server.
