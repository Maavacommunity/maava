PowerPoint-ist（/'pauəpɔintist/）, An online presentation application that replicates most of the commonly used features of MS PowerPoint, allowing for the editing and presentation of PPT online. It also supports AIPPT and local PPTX file preview.

PPTist has 9.2k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 1.8k times. PPTist is written mainly in Vue. It has been in active development since 2020. PPTist is available under the AGPL-3.0 license. Its main topics are ai, aippt, office, office-powerpoint.
