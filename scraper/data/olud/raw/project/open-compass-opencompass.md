OpenCompass is an LLM evaluation platform, supporting a wide range of models (Llama3, Mistral, InternLM2,GPT-4,LLaMa2, Qwen,GLM, Claude, etc) over 100+ datasets.

opencompass has 7.4k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 857 times. opencompass is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2023. opencompass is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are benchmark, chatgpt, evaluation, large-language-model.
