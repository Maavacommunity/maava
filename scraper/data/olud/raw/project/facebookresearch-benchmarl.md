📄 About

BenchMARL is a library for benchmarking Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL). BenchMARL allows to quickly compare different MARL algorithms, tasks, and models while being systematically grounded in its two core tenets: reproducibility and standardization.

BenchMARL has 641 stars on GitHub. It has been forked 134 times. BenchMARL is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2023. BenchMARL is available under the MIT license. Its main topics are benchmark, machine-learning, marl, multi-agent.
