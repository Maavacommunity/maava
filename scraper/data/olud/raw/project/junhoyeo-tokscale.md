What it doesTokscale is a CLI that reports your token usage and estimated cost across AI coding agents

Who it is forDevelopers using more than one AI coding tool who want one place to see what each is costing them

What it does not doIt doesn't cap, throttle, or route requests. Costs are list-price estimates, not your invoice
