📄 About

Amphion (/æmˈfaɪən/) is a toolkit for Audio, Music, and Speech Generation. Its purpose is to support reproducible research and help junior researchers and engineers get started in the field of audio, music, and speech generation research and development.

Amphion has 9.9k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 829 times. Amphion is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2023. Amphion is available under the MIT license. Its main topics are audio-generation, audio-synthesis, audioldm, audit.
