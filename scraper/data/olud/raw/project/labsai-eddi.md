📄 About

Config-driven engine that turns JSON into production-grade AI agents. Multi-agent orchestration, 12+ LLM providers, MCP/A2A protocols, RAG, persistent memory, and enterprise compliance (EU AI Act, GDPR, HIPAA). Built on Quarkus.

EDDI has 365 stars on GitHub. It has been forked 126 times. EDDI is written mainly in Java. It has been in active development since 2016. EDDI is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are a2a, ai-agents, ai-orchestration, chatbot.
