📄 About

Multi-Modal learning toolkit based on PaddlePaddle and PyTorch, supporting multiple applications such as multi-modal classification, cross-modal retrieval and image caption.

OMML has 477 stars on GitHub. It has been forked 75 times. OMML is written mainly in Python. It has been in active development since 2022. OMML is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are classification, crossmodal-retrieval, imagecaptioning, multimodal.
