📄 About

The Open Context Layer for Data and AI , OpenMetadata is the open platform for building trusted data context and business semantics for humans, AI assistants, and agents.

OpenMetadata has 15k stars on GitHub. It has been forked 2.3k times. OpenMetadata is written mainly in TypeScript. It has been in active development since 2021. OpenMetadata is available under the Apache-2.0 license. Its main topics are context, context-layer, data-catalog, data-collaboration.
