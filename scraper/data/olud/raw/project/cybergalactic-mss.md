📄 About

The Marine Systems Simulator (MSS) is software that supplements the textbook "Handbook of Marine Craft Hydrodynamics and Motion Control," 2nd Edition, by T. I. Fossen, published in 2021 by John Wiley & Sons Ltd.

MSS has 710 stars on GitHub. It has been forked 198 times. MSS is written mainly in MATLAB. It has been in active development since 2018. MSS is available under the MIT license. Its main topics are control-systems, gnc, guidance, marine.
