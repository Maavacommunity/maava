## Open-Source Alternatives to Perplexity

Perplexity answers questions with AI and cites live web sources, but it is a paid, closed product. Open-source AI search tools let you self-host the same experience — your queries stay private and you choose the model. Here are the best open-source alternatives to Perplexity, what each is best at, and how to run one yourself.

Updated regularly · curated by olud.ai

### Why choose an open-source alternative?

🔒

#### Privacy & control
Run models on your own machine or servers so your prompts and data never leave your control — no third party sees them.

💸

#### Lower cost
Run locally for free, or use a hosted option that is often far cheaper per token, with no monthly subscription.

🧩

#### Customization
Fine-tune on your own data, change behaviour, and integrate the tool deeply into your own products and workflows.

🔓

#### No vendor lock-in
The software is yours to keep. No surprise deprecations, no forced upgrades, no sudden price hikes pulling the rug out.

### How to self-host AI search
Most of these tools run as a single Docker container. Deploy it, connect a model, and you have a private answer engine in minutes.

#### 🐳 Docker
Deploy Perplexica (Vane) or Morphic with a single container on your own server or PC.

#### 🦙 Ollama
Connect a local model so the entire search-and-answer stack runs offline and private.

#### 🔍 SearXNG
The bundled meta-search engine fetches web results from dozens of engines without tracking you.

#### ☁️ Or a cheap API
Want faster answers? Plug in a low-cost hosted model instead of running one locally.

**Privacy note:** when self-hosted with a local model, your queries and history never leave your hardware — no tracking, no ad targeting, no data sold.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is there an open-source Perplexity?
Yes. Perplexica (rebranded to Vane in 2026) is the most popular open, self-hostable Perplexity alternative, with Morphic and Khoj as strong options.

#### Can I self-host AI search?
Yes. Most of these run as a Docker container you deploy on your own server or machine.

#### Are my searches private?
Self-hosted, yes — your queries and history stay on your hardware, with no tracking or ad targeting.

#### Do I need an API key?
No. Connect a local model via Ollama for a completely free, private setup, or use a cheap hosted API for faster answers.

#### Which is closest to Perplexity?
Perplexica (Vane) most closely reproduces the Perplexity workflow of question, web research, then cited answer.
