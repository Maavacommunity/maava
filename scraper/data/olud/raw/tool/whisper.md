### What is Whisper?

Whisper is OpenAI's open-weight speech-to-text model and reference implementation, widely used as the baseline for self-hosted transcription.

### Why people choose Whisper
- Strong multilingual accuracy
- Open weights under MIT
- The de-facto reference for STT

### FAQ

#### Is Whisper free?
Whisper is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Whisper locally?
Yes. Whisper is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Whisper?
Popular open-source alternatives include faster-whisper, WhisperX, Piper. See the comparisons above to choose.
