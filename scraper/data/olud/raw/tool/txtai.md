### What is txtai?

txtai is an all-in-one embeddings database for semantic search, LLM orchestration and RAG, bundling vector indexing, pipelines and workflows in one package.

### Why people choose txtai
- Vector search, pipelines and workflows together
- Runs fully locally
- Minimal dependencies

### FAQ

#### Is txtai free?
txtai is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run txtai locally?
Yes. txtai is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to txtai?
Popular open-source alternatives include LangChain, LlamaIndex, Haystack. See the comparisons above to choose.
