### What is Windmill?

Windmill turns Python, TypeScript or SQL scripts into workflows, schedules and internal apps, with AI steps as first-class citizens.

### Why people choose Windmill
- Scripts first, UI generated for you
- Very fast execution engine
- Self-hosted, no vendor lock-in

#### Is Windmill free?
Windmill is free and open-source (AGPL-3.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Windmill locally?
Yes. Windmill is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
