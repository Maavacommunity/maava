### What is MuJoCo?

MuJoCo simulates contact-rich dynamics fast and accurately. DeepMind open-sourced it, and it is now the default for reinforcement learning on robots.

### Why people choose MuJoCo
- Accurate contact dynamics at very high speed
- Free since DeepMind released it
- The de-facto standard in RL papers

### FAQ

#### Is MuJoCo free?
MuJoCo is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run MuJoCo locally?
Yes. MuJoCo is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to MuJoCo?
Popular open-source alternatives include Genesis, LeRobot, ArduPilot. See the comparisons above to choose.
