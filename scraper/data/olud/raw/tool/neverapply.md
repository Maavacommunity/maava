### What is NeverApply?

NeverApply is a commercial web app that reads job postings, rewrites a candidate's resume for each one, and submits the application on career sites and applicant tracking systems. It is hosted only, with a free tier of 10 applications. Note before using it: automated submission is against the terms of service of most major job boards and ATS platforms, including LinkedIn, Indeed and Workday, and can get a candidate's account suspended.

### Why people choose NeverApply
- Handles the whole chain: matching, tailoring, submitting
- Free tier of 10 applications, no card required
- Interface available in eight languages
