### What is Kokoro?

Kokoro is an 82-million-parameter TTS model that rivals far larger systems: near-instant synthesis, multiple voices and languages, deployable anywhere from servers to browsers.

### Why people choose Kokoro
- Remarkable quality at only 82M parameters
- Real-time synthesis even on CPU
- Permissive Apache license for products

### FAQ

#### Is Kokoro free?
Kokoro is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Kokoro locally?
Yes. Kokoro is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Kokoro?
Popular open-source alternatives include Whisper, faster-whisper, WhisperX. See the comparisons above to choose.
