### What is Khoj?

Khoj is a personal AI assistant that indexes your notes and documents (Markdown, Org, PDF, Notion) and lets you chat over them, self-hosted or in their cloud.

### Why people choose Khoj
- Deep integration with notes and PKM tools
- Semantic search plus chat over your corpus
- Self-hosted or managed, with local models

Khoj is free and open-source (AGPL-3.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Khoj locally?
Yes. Khoj is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
