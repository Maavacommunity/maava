### What is Habitat?

Habitat simulates indoor environments at thousands of frames per second so agents can learn navigation and object manipulation from vision.

### Why people choose Habitat
- Extremely fast photorealistic simulation
- Large library of scanned real homes
- The standard benchmark for embodied navigation

### FAQ

#### Is Habitat free?
Habitat is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Habitat locally?
Yes. Habitat is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Habitat?
