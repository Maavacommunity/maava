### What is Ragas?

Ragas scores RAG pipelines on faithfulness, answer relevance and context precision, turning "it feels better" into numbers.

### Why people choose Ragas
- Objective RAG quality metrics
- Catches hallucinations quantitatively
- Integrates with LangChain and LlamaIndex

### FAQ

#### Is Ragas free?
Ragas is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Ragas locally?
Yes. Ragas is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
