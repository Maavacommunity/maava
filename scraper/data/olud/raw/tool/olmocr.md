### What is olmOCR?

olmOCR from AI2 converts messy PDFs into clean linearised text at scale, preserving reading order across columns and figures.

### Why people choose olmOCR
- Handles multi-column academic PDFs
- Built for very large batches
- Preserves correct reading order
