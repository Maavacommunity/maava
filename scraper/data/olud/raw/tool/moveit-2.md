### What is MoveIt 2?

MoveIt is the reference stack for planning collision-free arm trajectories, grasping and kinematics on top of ROS 2.

### Why people choose MoveIt 2
- Battle-tested motion planning
- Deep ROS 2 integration
- Supports most commercial arms out of the box

Yes. MoveIt 2 is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
