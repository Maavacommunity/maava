### What is FlashRank?

FlashRank re-ranks retrieved passages with tiny cross-encoder models, sharply improving RAG answer quality at almost no cost.

### Why people choose FlashRank
- Big RAG quality gain for a few lines
- Tiny models, runs on CPU
- No API calls needed

### FAQ

#### Is FlashRank free?
FlashRank is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run FlashRank locally?
Yes. FlashRank is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
