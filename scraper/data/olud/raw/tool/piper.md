### What is Piper?

Piper is a fast, local neural text-to-speech engine that runs well even on low-power devices like the Raspberry Pi, with many voices.

### Why people choose Piper
- Fast enough for Raspberry Pi
- Many voices and languages
- Fully offline TTS

### FAQ

#### Is Piper free?
Piper is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Piper locally?
Yes. Piper is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
