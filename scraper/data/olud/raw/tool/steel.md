### What is Steel?

Steel is an open browser-as-API: launch controlled Chrome sessions for your agents with anti-bot handling, session management and a live debug view, self-hosted or cloud.

### Why people choose Steel
- Purpose-built browser sessions for agents
- Session reuse, proxies and debugging UI
- Self-hostable with Docker in minutes
