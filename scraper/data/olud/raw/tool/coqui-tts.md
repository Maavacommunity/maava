### What is Coqui TTS?

Coqui TTS is a battle-tested deep-learning toolkit for text-to-speech with many models and voice cloning, including the multilingual XTTS model.

### Why people choose Coqui TTS
- Many TTS models and languages
- Voice cloning with XTTS
- Widely used and documented

### FAQ

#### Is Coqui TTS free?
Coqui TTS is free and open-source (MPL-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Coqui TTS locally?
Yes. Coqui TTS is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
