### What is HunyuanVideo?

HunyuanVideo is Tencent's large open video generation model with cinematic quality, plus an ecosystem of acceleration and ComfyUI integrations.

### Why people choose HunyuanVideo
- Cinematic quality among open models
- Strong motion and prompt adherence
- Broad community tooling and ComfyUI support
