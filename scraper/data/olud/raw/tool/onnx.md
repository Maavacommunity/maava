### What is ONNX?

ONNX is the common format that lets a model trained in PyTorch run in a C++ runtime, on mobile, or on an edge accelerator.

### Why people choose ONNX
- Framework-neutral by design
- ONNX Runtime is fast on CPU and edge
- Backed by the whole industry

### FAQ

#### Is ONNX free?
ONNX is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run ONNX locally?
Yes. ONNX is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to ONNX?
Popular open-source alternatives include Dagster, TensorFlow, PyTorch. See the comparisons above to choose.
