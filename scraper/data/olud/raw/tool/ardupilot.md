### What is ArduPilot?

ArduPilot is the autopilot firmware flying a large share of the world's open drones, plus ground rovers, boats and submarines.

### Why people choose ArduPilot
- Supports aircraft, rovers, boats and submarines
- Enormous hardware compatibility
- Two decades of field testing

ArduPilot is free and open-source (GPL-3.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

Yes. ArduPilot is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
