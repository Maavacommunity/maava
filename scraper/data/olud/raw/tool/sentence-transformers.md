### What is Sentence Transformers?

Sentence Transformers is the reference library for computing text and image embeddings, and for fine-tuning your own embedding models.

### Why people choose Sentence Transformers
- The de-facto embeddings standard
- Hundreds of pretrained models
- Fine-tune your own embedder easily

### FAQ

#### Is Sentence Transformers free?
Sentence Transformers is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Sentence Transformers locally?
Yes. Sentence Transformers is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Sentence Transformers?
Popular open-source alternatives include LangChain, LlamaIndex, Haystack. See the comparisons above to choose.
