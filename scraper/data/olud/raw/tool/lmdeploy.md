### What is LMDeploy?

LMDeploy is a toolkit for compressing, quantizing and serving LLMs with high request throughput via its TurboMind engine.

### Why people choose LMDeploy
- High throughput via the TurboMind engine
- Built-in quantization and compression
- Efficient KV-cache management

LMDeploy is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

Yes. LMDeploy is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
