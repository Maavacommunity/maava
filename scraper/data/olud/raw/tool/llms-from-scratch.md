### What is LLMs from Scratch?

Sebastian Raschka's companion repository to "Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)": you implement attention, a transformer, pretraining and fine-tuning yourself, in plain PyTorch.

### Why people choose LLMs from Scratch
- You build every component yourself — no black boxes
- Runs on a laptop, no cluster needed
- The clearest explanation of attention anywhere

### FAQ

#### Is LLMs from Scratch free?
LLMs from Scratch is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run LLMs from Scratch locally?
Yes. LLMs from Scratch is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
