### What is Krita AI Diffusion?

This plugin brings Stable Diffusion and Flux directly into Krita: inpaint on layers, refine selections and live-paint with AI while keeping full artistic control.

### Why people choose Krita AI Diffusion
- True layer-based inpainting in a pro painting app
- Live painting mode with instant AI feedback
- Uses a local ComfyUI backend — nothing leaves your machine
