### What is ML for Beginners?

A 12-week, 26-lesson curriculum from Microsoft covering classical machine learning with scikit-learn, built around hands-on projects rather than theory dumps.

### Why people choose ML for Beginners
- Project-based: you build things from lesson one
- Quizzes and assignments, not just reading
- Available in dozens of languages

### FAQ

#### Is ML for Beginners free?
ML for Beginners is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run ML for Beginners locally?
Yes. ML for Beginners is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

Popular open-source alternatives include Virgilio, AI for Beginners, Generative AI for Beginners. See the comparisons above to choose.
