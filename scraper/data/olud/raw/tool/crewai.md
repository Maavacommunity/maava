### What is CrewAI?

CrewAI is a framework for orchestrating role-playing autonomous agents that collaborate as a crew to complete complex tasks, independent of LangChain.

### Why people choose CrewAI
- Intuitive role-based agent crews
- Standalone, no LangChain dependency
- Fast-growing community and templates
