### What is IP-Adapter?

IP-Adapter lets diffusion models take an image as a prompt, transferring style or subject without retraining the base model.

### Why people choose IP-Adapter
- Image prompting without fine-tuning
- Works with existing SD and SDXL models
- Strong style and face consistency
