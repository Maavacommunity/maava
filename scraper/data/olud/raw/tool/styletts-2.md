#### Is StyleTTS 2 free?
StyleTTS 2 is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run StyleTTS 2 locally?
Yes. StyleTTS 2 is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
