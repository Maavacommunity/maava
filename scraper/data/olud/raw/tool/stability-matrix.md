### What is Stability Matrix?

Stability Matrix installs and updates ComfyUI, A1111, Forge and friends side by side, with shared model folders, checkpoint browsing and one-click launches.

### Why people choose Stability Matrix
- Installs A1111, ComfyUI, Forge & more in one click
- Shared model library across all interfaces
- Built-in CivitAI browser for checkpoints and LoRAs
