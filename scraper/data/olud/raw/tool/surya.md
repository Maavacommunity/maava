### What is Surya?

Surya is a modern OCR toolkit providing text recognition in 90+ languages plus layout analysis, reading order and table detection.

### Why people choose Surya
- 90+ languages with strong accuracy
- Layout, reading order and table detection
- Runs locally on modest GPUs
