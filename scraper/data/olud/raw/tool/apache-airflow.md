Airflow schedules the pipelines that feed your models — the standard orchestrator in data engineering.

### Why people choose Apache Airflow
- The industry standard, with connectors for everything
- Clear visibility into what ran and what broke
- Huge community and plugin ecosystem

### FAQ

#### Is Apache Airflow free?
Apache Airflow is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Apache Airflow locally?
Yes. Apache Airflow is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
