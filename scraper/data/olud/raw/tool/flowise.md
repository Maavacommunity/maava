### What is Flowise?

Flowise is a drag-and-drop builder for LLM apps and agents, letting you assemble chains, tools and RAG visually on top of LangChain primitives.

### Why people choose Flowise
- Fast drag-and-drop flow building
- Built on familiar LangChain primitives
- Quick to prototype chatbots and agents
