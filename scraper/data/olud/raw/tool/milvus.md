### What is Milvus?

Milvus is a cloud-native vector database built for massive scale, with multiple index types and a distributed architecture for billions of vectors.

### Why people choose Milvus
- Scales to billions of vectors
- Many index types for tuning
- Cloud-native, distributed architecture

### FAQ

#### Is Milvus free?
Milvus is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Milvus locally?
Yes. Milvus is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
