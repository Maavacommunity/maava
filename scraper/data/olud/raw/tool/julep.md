### What is Julep?

Julep provides a backend for long-running, stateful agents with persistent memory and scheduled tasks, so your agents survive restarts.

### Why people choose Julep
- Persistent state across sessions
- Scheduled and long-running tasks
- Self-hostable backend

### FAQ

#### Is Julep free?
Julep is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Julep locally?
Yes. Julep is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
