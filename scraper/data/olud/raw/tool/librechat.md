### What is LibreChat?

LibreChat is an open-source ChatGPT clone that unifies many AI providers behind one interface, with conversation search, presets, plugins and multi-user auth.

### Why people choose LibreChat
- Unifies many providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, local) in one UI
- Conversation search, presets and plugins
- Active development and broad MIT license

#### Is LibreChat free?
LibreChat is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run LibreChat locally?
Yes. LibreChat is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
