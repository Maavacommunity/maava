### What is Isaac Lab?

Isaac Lab runs thousands of simulated robots in parallel on a single GPU, cutting reinforcement-learning training from days to minutes.

### Why people choose Isaac Lab
- Thousands of parallel environments on one GPU
- Photorealistic sensors for perception training
- Direct path from simulation to real hardware

### FAQ

#### Is Isaac Lab free?
Isaac Lab is free and open-source (BSD-3-Clause license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Isaac Lab locally?
Yes. Isaac Lab is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
