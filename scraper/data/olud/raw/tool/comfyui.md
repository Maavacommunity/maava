### What is ComfyUI?

ComfyUI is a node-graph interface for diffusion models that exposes every step of the generation pipeline, enabling complex, reproducible and shareable workflows.

### Why people choose ComfyUI
- Total control via a visual node graph
- Reproducible, shareable workflows
- First to support new models and techniques
