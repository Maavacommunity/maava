### What is Dify?

Dify is an open-source AI infrastructure platform for building, deploying and operating LLM applications and agents, with RAG pipelines, visual workflows and observability built in. Its publisher also sells a hosted cloud and an enterprise edition.

### Why people choose Dify
- Full LLMOps: build, deploy and monitor
- Visual workflows plus built-in RAG
- Production-oriented with observability

### FAQ

#### Is Dify free?
Dify is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost. Its publisher also sells a commercial edition; the open-source edition stays free to self-host.

#### Can I run Dify locally?
Yes. Dify is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Dify?
Popular open-source alternatives include Flowise, Langflow, n8n. See the comparisons above to choose.
