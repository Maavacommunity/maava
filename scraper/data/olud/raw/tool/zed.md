### What is Zed?

Zed is a GPU-accelerated editor written in Rust by the Atom creators, with built-in AI edit prediction, agentic editing and real-time collaboration.

### Why people choose Zed
- Startlingly fast — GPU-rendered, Rust core
- Agentic editing and edit prediction built in
- Real-time collaboration without extensions
