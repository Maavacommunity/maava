### What is BentoML?

BentoML packages models, code and dependencies into a reproducible artifact and serves it as a scalable API, with adaptive batching built in.

### Why people choose BentoML
- Reproducible model packaging
- Adaptive batching out of the box
- Deploys to Docker, K8s or cloud

### FAQ

#### Is BentoML free?
BentoML is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run BentoML locally?
Yes. BentoML is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to BentoML?
Popular open-source alternatives include vLLM, TGI, SGLang. See the comparisons above to choose.
