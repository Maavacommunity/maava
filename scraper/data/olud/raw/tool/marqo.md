### What is Marqo?

Marqo handles embedding generation and vector search together, so you send text or images and it does the rest — no separate embedding step.

### Why people choose Marqo
- Embeddings generated for you
- Text and image search out of the box
- No separate embedding pipeline

### FAQ

#### Is Marqo free?
Marqo is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Marqo locally?
Yes. Marqo is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
