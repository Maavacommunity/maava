### What is FramePack?

FramePack is a next-frame prediction approach that generates long videos progressively with low, constant VRAM use, making video generation practical on consumer GPUs.

### Why people choose FramePack
- Constant VRAM regardless of clip length
- Practical on consumer GPUs
- From the author of Fooocus and ControlNet
