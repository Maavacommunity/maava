### What is TRL?

TRL is Hugging Face's library for post-training and aligning language models with supervised fine-tuning, DPO and reinforcement learning methods like PPO.

### Why people choose TRL
- SFT, DPO and PPO in one library
- Integrates with PEFT and Accelerate
- Maintained by Hugging Face

### FAQ

#### Is TRL free?
TRL is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run TRL locally?
Yes. TRL is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to TRL?
Popular open-source alternatives include Unsloth, Axolotl, LLaMA-Factory. See the comparisons above to choose.
