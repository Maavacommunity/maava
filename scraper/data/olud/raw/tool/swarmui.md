### What is SwarmUI?

SwarmUI pairs an accessible generate-and-go interface with a full ComfyUI backend: beginners click, power users open the node graph, and multi-GPU grids come free.

### Why people choose SwarmUI
- Simple tab for beginners, ComfyUI graph one click away
- First-class support for new models (Flux, SD3.5, video)
- Multi-backend and multi-GPU orchestration built in
