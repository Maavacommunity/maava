### What is Gymnasium?

Gymnasium is the maintained successor to OpenAI Gym: one API that every RL algorithm and environment speaks.

### Why people choose Gymnasium
- The interface the whole RL ecosystem implements
- Dozens of environments included
- Actively maintained, unlike the original Gym

### FAQ

#### Is Gymnasium free?
Gymnasium is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Gymnasium locally?
Yes. Gymnasium is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Gymnasium?
Popular open-source alternatives include Genesis, LeRobot, ArduPilot. See the comparisons above to choose.
