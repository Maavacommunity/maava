### What is Kotaemon?

Kotaemon is a clean, open-source RAG interface for chatting with your documents, with citations, multi-user support and a customizable pipeline for developers.

### Why people choose Kotaemon
- Answers with inline citations and previews
- Both end-user UI and developer framework
- Gradio-based, easy to theme and extend

Yes. Kotaemon is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
