### What is Open WebUI?

Open WebUI is a self-hosted, ChatGPT-style web interface that runs on top of Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible API, with chat history, model switching and document RAG.

### Why people choose Open WebUI
- Polished ChatGPT-style UI with multi-user support
- Built-in document RAG and model switching
- Works with Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint

### FAQ

#### Is Open WebUI free?
Open WebUI is free and open-source (BSD-3-Clause license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Open WebUI locally?
Yes. Open WebUI is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Open WebUI?
Popular open-source alternatives include LibreChat, Lobe Chat, AnythingLLM. See the comparisons above to choose.
