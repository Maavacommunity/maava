### What is Gazebo?

Gazebo simulates robots with their sensors and environment — the classic testing ground before deploying to real hardware.

### Why people choose Gazebo
- Realistic sensor simulation
- Tight ROS integration
- Decades of robotics use behind it

### FAQ

#### Is Gazebo free?
Gazebo is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Gazebo locally?
Yes. Gazebo is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
