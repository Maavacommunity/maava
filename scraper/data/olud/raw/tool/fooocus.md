### What is Fooocus?

Fooocus is a streamlined image generator that hides the complexity of Stable Diffusion behind a Midjourney-like experience, optimizing quality with minimal settings.

### Why people choose Fooocus
- Midjourney-like simplicity, almost no settings
- Smart defaults that produce strong results
- Minimal setup, runs on modest GPUs
