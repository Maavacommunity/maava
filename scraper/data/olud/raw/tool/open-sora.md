### What is Open-Sora?

Open-Sora is an open effort to democratize video generation, providing trainable, runnable text-to-video models and the full training pipeline.

### Why people choose Open-Sora
- Fully open: weights, code and training pipeline
- Text-to-video and image-to-video
- Active research community

### FAQ

#### Is Open-Sora free?
Open-Sora is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Open-Sora locally?
Yes. Open-Sora is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
