### What is SearXNG?

SearXNG is a privacy-respecting meta-search engine aggregating dozens of sources, widely used as the retrieval layer behind self-hosted AI answer engines.

### Why people choose SearXNG
- Aggregates dozens of engines, zero tracking
- The standard retrieval layer for local AI search
- Battle-tested, easy Docker deployment
