### What is Marker?

Marker converts PDFs and office documents to clean Markdown, JSON and HTML with high accuracy, handling tables, equations and multi-column layouts.

### Why people choose Marker
- High-accuracy PDF to Markdown, tables included
- Handles equations and complex layouts
- Fast batch conversion on GPU or CPU
