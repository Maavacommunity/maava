### What is Plandex?

Plandex is a terminal-based AI coding agent designed for large tasks and real codebases, with a plan-first workflow, diff review and cumulative context management.

### Why people choose Plandex
- Plan-first workflow with reviewable diffs
- Handles very large contexts across files
- Terminal-native with git-friendly output
