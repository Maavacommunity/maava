### What is OpenCV?

OpenCV is the toolbox for reading, transforming and analysing images and video — the layer beneath most vision pipelines, including the deep ones.

### Why people choose OpenCV
- Two decades of optimised vision primitives
- Runs everywhere, from servers to microcontrollers
- Bindings for Python, C++, Java and more

### FAQ

#### Is OpenCV free?
OpenCV is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run OpenCV locally?
Yes. OpenCV is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to OpenCV?
Popular open-source alternatives include Dagster, TensorFlow, PyTorch. See the comparisons above to choose.
