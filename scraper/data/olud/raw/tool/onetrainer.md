### What is OneTrainer?

OneTrainer is a unified training suite for Stable Diffusion and FLUX models, covering LoRA, embeddings and full fine-tunes behind one consistent UI.

### Why people choose OneTrainer
- LoRA, embeddings and full fine-tunes in one tool
- Supports the newest diffusion architectures
- Memory-efficient training options
