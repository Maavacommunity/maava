### What is AI for Beginners?

Microsoft's 12-week AI curriculum covering the history of AI, symbolic approaches, neural networks, computer vision and NLP, with runnable notebooks throughout.

### Why people choose AI for Beginners
- Covers the full breadth of AI, not just the trendy parts
- Works with both TensorFlow and PyTorch
- Free and genuinely well-structured

### FAQ

#### Is AI for Beginners free?
AI for Beginners is free and open-source (MIT license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run AI for Beginners locally?
Yes. AI for Beginners is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to AI for Beginners?
Popular open-source alternatives include Virgilio, ML for Beginners, Generative AI for Beginners. See the comparisons above to choose.
