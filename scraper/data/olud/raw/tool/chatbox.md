### What is Chatbox?

Chatbox is a cross-platform desktop client for LLMs, letting you chat with local or hosted models, store conversations locally and use it offline.

### Why people choose Chatbox
- Native desktop app for all platforms
- Local conversation storage and privacy
- Works with local and cloud models

### FAQ

#### Is Chatbox free?
Chatbox is free and open-source (GPL-3.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Chatbox locally?
Yes. Chatbox is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to Chatbox?
Popular open-source alternatives include Open WebUI, LibreChat, Lobe Chat. See the comparisons above to choose.
