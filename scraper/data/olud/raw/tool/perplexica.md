### What is Perplexica?

Perplexica is an open-source AI answer engine that searches the web and answers with cited sources, running entirely on your infrastructure with local model support.

### Why people choose Perplexica
- Answers with cited sources like Perplexity
- Works with local models via Ollama
- Fully self-hosted and private

Yes. Perplexica is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
