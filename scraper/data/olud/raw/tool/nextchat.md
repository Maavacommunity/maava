### What is NextChat?

NextChat is one of the most-starred open chat UIs: a fast ChatGPT-like interface for OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and local endpoints, deployable on Vercel in a minute or as a desktop app.

### Why people choose NextChat
- One-click Vercel deploy and tiny footprint
- Prompt masks and templates built in
- Works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, local included
