### What is Axolotl?

Axolotl is a config-driven fine-tuning framework supporting many model families and training techniques through simple YAML files.

### Why people choose Axolotl
- Reproducible YAML-based training configs
- Supports many models and techniques (LoRA, QLoRA)
- Multi-GPU and cloud friendly

### FAQ

#### Is Axolotl free?
Axolotl is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Axolotl locally?
Yes. Axolotl is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
