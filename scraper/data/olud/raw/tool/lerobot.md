### What is LeRobot?

LeRobot brings pretrained models, datasets and simulation environments for real-world robotics into one Python library — the closest thing the field has to a Transformers moment.

### Why people choose LeRobot
- Pretrained policies and shared datasets on the Hub
- Works with cheap off-the-shelf arms, not just lab hardware
- Backed by an active community and weekly releases

### FAQ

#### Is LeRobot free?
LeRobot is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run LeRobot locally?
Yes. LeRobot is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to LeRobot?
Popular open-source alternatives include Genesis, ArduPilot, MuJoCo. See the comparisons above to choose.
