### What is Dagster?

Dagster models pipelines around the data they produce rather than the tasks they run — which makes lineage and testing far easier than in Airflow.

### Why people choose Dagster
- Asset-centric model with built-in lineage
- Local development that actually works
- Strong typing and testing story

### FAQ

#### Is Dagster free?
Dagster is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run Dagster locally?
Yes. Dagster is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.
