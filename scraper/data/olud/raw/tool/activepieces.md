### What is Activepieces?

Activepieces is an open-source automation platform (a Zapier alternative) with hundreds of connectors and first-class AI steps, self-hostable via Docker.

### Why people choose Activepieces
- Hundreds of connectors, no-code editor
- AI steps as first-class citizens
- MIT core, simple Docker self-hosting
