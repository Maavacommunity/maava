### What is DVC?

DVC versions the data and the models that Git cannot hold, keeping the whole pipeline reproducible from a commit hash.

### Why people choose DVC
- Works alongside Git, not against it
- Storage-agnostic (S3, GCS, SSH, local)
- Makes pipelines reproducible by construction

### FAQ

#### Is DVC free?
DVC is free and open-source (Apache-2.0 license), so you can use, self-host and modify it at no cost.

#### Can I run DVC locally?
Yes. DVC is designed to run on your own machine or server, keeping your data private.

#### What is the best alternative to DVC?
Popular open-source alternatives include Dagster, TensorFlow, PyTorch. See the comparisons above to choose.
