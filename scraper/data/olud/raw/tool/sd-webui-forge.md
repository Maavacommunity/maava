### What is SD WebUI Forge?

Stable Diffusion WebUI Forge is a performance-focused fork of AUTOMATIC1111 that speeds up generation and lowers VRAM use while keeping the familiar interface.

### Why people choose SD WebUI Forge
- Faster generation, lower VRAM
- Familiar A1111 interface and extensions
- Great for modest GPUs
