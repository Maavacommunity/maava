### What is MetaGPT?

MetaGPT assigns roles like product manager, architect and engineer to multiple LLM agents that collaborate to turn one line of requirements into code, docs and diagrams.

### Why people choose MetaGPT
- Role-based multi-agent collaboration
- Generates code, docs and diagrams together
- Large, active community
