### What is MinerU?

MinerU extracts text, tables, formulas and images from PDFs into Markdown or JSON, handling scientific documents particularly well.

### Why people choose MinerU
- Extracts formulas as LaTeX
- Strong table structure recovery
- Handles scanned documents
